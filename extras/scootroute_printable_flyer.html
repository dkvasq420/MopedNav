Be One of the First 12 People to Use
Oahu's First Moped Navigation App!

ScootRoute routes moped and scooter riders around Oahu using surface streets only — never sending you onto H1, H2 or H3 freeways where mopeds aren't allowed. We need 12 local riders to test the app for 14 days before it launches on Google Play. No experience needed — just ride and give us feedback!

🚫 Zero Freeway Routing Automatically avoids H1, H2 & H3 — every single route
🎙️ Voice Navigation Turn-by-turn spoken directions like Google Maps
🛰️ Satellite + Street View Switch between map styles with one tap
⚠️ Hazard Reporting Report potholes, flooding, road closures & more
🏍️ Moped Rentals Find and book moped rentals across Oahu
📊 Ride Tracking Track your rides, distance & route history
